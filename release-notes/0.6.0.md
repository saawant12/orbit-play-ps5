# Orbit Play 0.6.0 (Beta)

Orbit Play is a free, open-source Remote Play app for jailbroken PS5s on Mac, Windows and Linux. Stream without a PSN sign-in, including from offline PS5 accounts that have never been linked to PSN. Account setup, the account ID, the Remote Play PIN, pairing and streaming all happen in one desktop app, so there's no base64 account-ID conversion, PIN tool or separate activation payload to run.

**A couple of improvements and fixes.**

- **Update from the app.** When a new version is out, choose **Update now** at the bottom of the main menu. Orbit Play downloads it, checks it against the release's published checksums, installs it in place of this copy and restarts. Your pairings and settings stay. This works from 0.6.0 on, so updating to 0.6.0 itself is still a download.
- **Replace helper on PS5.** When the PS5 runs another helper version, **Console setup** offers **Replace helper on PS5**. It stops the old helper and starts the one included with the app, then you enter the new trust code. Helpers from before 0.6.0 are stopped through Payload Manager.
- **Type on the PS5.** When the PS5 asks for text, such as a search, a text box opens in the player. Type with your computer's keyboard and press **Enter** to send it, or **Esc** to switch to the PS5's own keyboard.
- **Fit, fill or stretch.** Choose how the picture fills a window or screen of another shape under **Playback**, or press **B** in the player to switch.
- **Turn the PS5's sound off.** Press **N** or choose **Sound** in the player bar to stop playing the PS5's sound on your computer, and set the default under **Playback**.
- **Works with Payload Manager 0.6.** **Start helper on PS5** works again with Payload Manager 0.6 and newer, also when the helper is already stored on the PS5.
- **Its own player app on a Mac.** The player now runs as its own app, **Orbit Play Player**, apart from the main Orbit Play window.

## Install on your computer

Choose the package for your computer from **Assets**: the macOS DMG for Apple Silicon Macs, the Windows x64 ZIP, or the Linux x64 or arm64 AppImage. Windows 11 on ARM uses the x64 ZIP. See the installation guide above for requirements and first-launch permissions.

**You only need to download the desktop app.** It includes the player and the **0.6.0 PS5 helper**, and can start the helper for you. The standalone `orbit-play-0.6.0-ps5.elf` in Assets is optional, for those who prefer to load it themselves.

## Start on your PS5

Load your jailbreak, open Payload Manager or enable an ELF loader, and stay logged into the PS5 profile you want to use. In Orbit Play, enter the console's IP address and choose **Start helper on PS5**. Enter the six-digit trust code shown on the PS5, then pair and play. If the profile needs it, Orbit Play offers account setup before pairing.

Orbit Play has been tested on PS5 firmware 13.60 with Payload Manager v0.5.2 and v0.6.3. Other firmware may work but is untested.

## Update from an earlier version

Stop your stream, quit Orbit Play and replace the computer app: on a Mac, replace it in Applications; on Windows, extract the new ZIP into a fresh folder; on Linux, use the new AppImage. Open **Console setup** and choose **Find console**. When it reports the earlier helper, choose **Replace helper on PS5**, then enter the new trust code. If Payload Manager isn't running on the PS5, stop the old helper yourself, or fully restart the PS5 and reload the jailbreak, then choose **Start helper on PS5**.

From 0.6.0 on, choose **Update now** when a new version is out. Your saved pairings, playback settings, controls and play away from home remain.

For account setup, we recommend a separate PS5 profile with no saves: creating a local account ID may make existing saves inaccessible. [Account setup and save risks](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-play-ps5/blob/v0.6.0/guides/account-setup.md).
