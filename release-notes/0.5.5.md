# Orbit Play 0.5.5 (Beta)

Orbit Play is a free, open-source Remote Play app for jailbroken PS5s on Mac, Windows and Linux. Stream without a PSN sign-in, including from offline PS5 accounts that have never been linked to PSN. Account setup, the account ID, the Remote Play PIN, pairing and streaming all happen in one desktop app, so there's no base64 account-ID conversion, PIN tool or separate activation payload to run.

**Performance improvements and bug fixes.**

- **Controller sticks fixed.** Both DualSense sticks now move characters, cameras and menu selections on Mac, Windows and Linux.
- **Motion controls corrected.** Orbit Play now sends the DualSense's tilting and turning the way the PS5 expects, for games with motion controls such as gyro aiming.
- **Connect your controller any time.** Turn it on or plug it in during the stream, and the player picks it up within a second, also after a dropped Bluetooth connection. With more than one controller connected, the player uses the DualSense.
- **Full screen.** Choose **Full screen** in the player bar or press **F11** (**Ctrl+Cmd+F** on a Mac).
- **Keep streaming while someone plays on the PS5.** Orbit Play no longer ends your stream when someone logs in on the PS5 with their own profile and controller, as long as your profile stays logged in, so you can watch on your computer or share the stream.
- **Your screen stays on.** Your computer and its screen no longer go to sleep during a stream you play with only a controller.
- **Clearer codes.** Codes and addresses you type use a clearer font, so 5 and S look different.
- **PS5 helper fixes.** Getting an away code on the PS5 no longer holds up the helper, and the PS5's page names the app's **Not at home? Set up from anywhere** button.

## Install on your computer

Choose the package for your computer from **Assets**: the macOS DMG for Apple Silicon Macs, the Windows x64 ZIP, or the Linux x64 or arm64 AppImage. Windows 11 on ARM uses the x64 ZIP. See the installation guide above for requirements and first-launch permissions.

**You only need to download the desktop app.** It includes the player and the **0.5.5 PS5 helper**, and can start the helper for you. The standalone `orbit-play-0.5.5-ps5.elf` in Assets is optional, for those who prefer to load it themselves.

## Start on your PS5

Load your jailbreak, open Payload Manager or enable an ELF loader, and stay logged into the PS5 profile you want to use. In Orbit Play, enter the console's IP address and choose **Start helper on PS5**. Enter the six-digit trust code shown on the PS5, then pair and play. If the profile needs it, Orbit Play offers account setup before pairing.

Orbit Play has been tested on PS5 firmware 13.60 with Relapse and Payload Manager v0.5.2. Other firmware may work but is untested.

## Update from an earlier version

Stop your stream, quit Orbit Play and replace the computer app. Stop the old helper in Payload Manager, then choose **Start helper on PS5** in the new app. Replacing the ELF file alone does not stop a running helper. If it cannot be stopped, fully restart the PS5 and reload the jailbreak first.

Your saved pairings, playback settings, controls and play away from home remain. Enter the new trust code when asked.

For account setup, we recommend a separate PS5 profile with no saves: creating a local account ID may make existing saves inaccessible. [Account setup and save risks](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-play-ps5/blob/v0.5.5/guides/account-setup.md).
