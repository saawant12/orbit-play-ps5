# Orbit Play 0.3.0 (Beta)

Orbit Play is a Remote Play app for jailbroken PS5s on Mac, Windows and Linux. Play over your local network without PSN sign-in, including with local PS5 accounts that have never connected to PSN. It brings account setup, automatic account-ID and PIN handling, pairing and streaming into one desktop app.

**Start the PS5 helper from your computer, without downloading or sending the ELF yourself.**

- **Start from Orbit Play.** Open **Console setup → Start helper on PS5** to send and launch the included helper through Payload Manager or your ELF loader.
- **Pick up where you left off.** If the matching helper is already running, Orbit Play connects to it. If an older version is running, it tells you to stop it before updating.
- **Use your loader settings.** Change the Payload Manager or ELF-loader port under **Profile & connection options**. Orbit Play remembers the ports after connecting.
- **More reliable helper startup.** Orbit Play checks whether the helper started when a loader's reply is interrupted, and no longer waits for the ELF loader to disconnect before connecting to the helper.

[Install on Mac, Windows or Linux](../guides/installation.md) · [Connect your PS5](../guides/getting-started.md) · [PS5 helper guide](../guides/ps5-helper.md)

## Install on your computer

Choose the package for your computer from **Assets**: the macOS DMG for Apple Silicon, Windows x64 ZIP, or Linux x64/arm64 AppImage. Windows 11 on ARM uses the x64 ZIP. See the installation guide above for requirements and first-launch permissions.

**You only need to download the desktop app.** It includes the player and **0.3.0 PS5 helper**, and can start the helper for you. The standalone `orbit-play-0.3.0-ps5.elf` in Assets is optional, for those who prefer to load it themselves.

## Start on your PS5

Load your jailbreak, open Payload Manager or enable an ELF loader, and stay logged into the PS5 profile you want to use. In Orbit Play, enter the console's IP address and choose **Start helper on PS5**. Enter the six-digit code shown on the PS5, then pair and play.

## Update from an earlier version

Stop your stream, quit Orbit Play and replace the computer app. Stop the old helper in Payload Manager, then choose **Start helper on PS5** in the new app. Replacing the ELF file alone does not stop a running helper. If it cannot be stopped, fully restart the PS5 and reload the jailbreak first.

Your saved pairings and playback settings remain. Enter the new trust code when asked.

**Local network only:** keep your computer and PS5 on the same network, with the PS5 awake. For account setup, we recommend a separate PS5 profile with no saves: creating a local account ID may make existing saves inaccessible. [Account setup and save risks](../guides/account-setup.md).
